Projects

Power BI dashboards, each told as problem, process and insight. Open the live versions to interact with them.

Power BI

Smart Touch Report

The Problem: Order, cost and revenue data for a retailer selling bikes, accessories and clothing across Saudi cities was split between SQL and Excel sources. There was no single view of profit by product, customer or region.

Fragmented sources

The Process: Combined the SQL and Excel data, cleaned and transformed it in Power Query, and modeled the table relationships. Wrote DAX measures for orders, cost, revenue, profit and month-over-month change, then built slicers for gender, category, subcategory, region and date.

SQL + Excel Power Query DAX Map Visual

The Insight: 82K orders generated $38.19M revenue and $16.59M profit over 2019–2020. Accessories drove 40K orders, nearly double Bikes or Clothing. December 2020 closed at 4,066 orders, up 54.78% on the previous month.

Power BI · JX Tech Studio

Invoi: Invoice Operations Dashboard

The Problem: Built for JX Tech Studio, whose thousands of invoices from dozens of vendors had no shared view. It was hard to see total processed volume, which vendors mattered most, or how volume was trending over time.

No operational view

The Process: Modeled the invoice data with year and quarter attributes. Wrote DAX KPIs for processed volume, invoice count and average invoice value, ranked vendors by volume, and added a vendor slicer, a date-range filter and a detail table showing payment status.

DAX KPIs Vendor Ranking Trend Analysis

The Insight: $489.05M was processed across 10K invoices (average $48.91K) between 2024 and 2026. The top 15 vendors fall in a narrow $6.7M–$7.3M band, so no single vendor dominates and concentration risk is low.

Power BI

Financial & Sales Report

The Problem: Sales transactions across several countries, products and customer segments needed a profit-and-loss view. Leadership wanted to compare performance with the prior year and see where discounts were eroding margin.

No P&L visibility

The Process: Built a two-page report with DAX measures for gross sales, discounts, COGS, profit and margin. Added time-intelligence measures for prior-year sales and growth, a quarterly P&L summary matrix, and slicers for country, product and date.

P&L Measures Time Intelligence Segmentation

The Insight: $118.73M in sales produced $16.89M profit (14.23% margin). The Government segment led with $53M in sales, Paseo was the most profitable product at $4.8M, and sales peaked in October at $21.67M.
